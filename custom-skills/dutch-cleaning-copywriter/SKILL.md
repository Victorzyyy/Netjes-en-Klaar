# Dutch Cleaning Copywriter

Use this skill to make Dutch copy sound clear, professional, direct, and natural.

## Voice
- Simple Dutch. No corporate fog.
- Professional but human.
- Confident, not exaggerated.
- Short sentences. Concrete promises.

## Words To Prefer
- schoonmaak
- planning
- vaste ronde
- eenmalige schoonmaak
- oplevering
- wisselschoonmaak
- tijdvak
- takenlijst
- aanspreekpunt
- locatie
- team
- terugkoppeling

## Avoid
- generieke ronde
- lazy use of ontzorgen
- naadloze ervaring, innovatieve oplossingen, next-level service
- overly formal phrasing a Dutch client would not say out loud

## Service Copy Patterns
- Kantoor: representative workspaces, sanitation, pantry, meeting rooms, fixed checklist.
- Vakantiepark: wisseldagen, bungalows/chalets, check-in timing, linen/inventory check if offered.
- Events: before/during/after event cleaning, fast switching.
- Bouwoplevering: dust-free handover, renovation/new build, detail cleaning.

## CTA Patterns
- Plan een korte intake
- Vraag planning aan
- Bel direct
- Bespreek je locatie
- Stuur je aanvraag

## Process Copy Pattern
- Intake: what, where, when.
- Planning: team, time window, task list.
- Oplevering: feedback, follow-up, recurring option.
