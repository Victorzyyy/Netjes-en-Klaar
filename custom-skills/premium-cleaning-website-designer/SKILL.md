# Premium Cleaning Website Designer

Use this skill to make a cleaning/facility-service website feel premium, fresh, trustworthy, and intentionally designed.

## Design Position
- Aim for calm premium service, not loud SaaS, not childish cleaning cartoon, not generic AI landing page.
- Prefer fewer stronger sections over many widgets.
- Let the design breathe: generous vertical rhythm, clear section purpose, and restrained decorative elements.
- Preserve the fresh cleaning palette: misty blue, soft green, white/glass, deep ink. Avoid random accent colors.
- Avoid heavy white cards everywhere when the background already has atmosphere.

## Layout Rules
- Design mobile-first, then expand to tablet/desktop.
- Center hero and key statement sections when the page needs calm confidence.
- Use left/right layouts only when there is real supporting content, not filler.
- On mobile, avoid three-column concepts becoming cramped cards; prefer stacked rows or short centered blocks.
- Keep max line length comfortable: headlines balanced, body copy around 55-75 characters when possible.

## Section Rhythm
- Hero: one strong message, one supporting sentence, one primary CTA, one secondary CTA max.
- Services: show how the company thinks, not just boxes of service names.
- Process: should feel like a clear flow, not a boring numbered list.
- Trust: use proof, operations, clarity, and reliability; avoid generic badges.
- Contact: make the next action easy and visible.

## Anti AI-Slop Checklist
- Does every section have a job?
- Are there too many cards, icons, blobs, bubbles, or effects?
- Is the mobile version intentionally designed, not merely stacked?
- Is text readable over all decorative layers?
- Does the page look like a real business, not a template demo?
